# Neal Nichols Jr. and Geography Gameshow: online reference audit

Research date: November 8, 2026.

Located **35 distinct online reference items** after consolidation: **4 identifiable newspaper articles** (3 with accessible article text and 1 indexed lead with inaccessible publisher text), **1 newspaper photo-coverage item**, **1 magazine article**, and **29 other references**. Of the 35, 34 have supporting content in a retrieved page/PDF or a search engine’s indexed text. This is a documented minimum from this search, not an exhaustive lifetime total or a count of independent endorsements.

The numbered register below is the basis of the count. An item is a distinct article, dated school publication, institutional record, blog account, directory entry, or event notice. Different years of a school’s newsletter count separately. Mirrors, tag pages, alternate URLs, and repeated event announcements are consolidated. Separately written reporting of the same event can count separately. No inaccessible article is represented as having been read in full.

Searches covered Neal Nichols / Neal Nichols Jr., Neil Nichols / Neal Nicholls, Geography Game Show / Geography Gameshow, domain-specific newspaper and school searches, and U.S., British, New Zealand, Australian, Japanese, Cook Islands and Nepal connections. Search-engine crawl dates were not treated as publication dates. Social-media screenshots already in the project are not new internet discoveries.
